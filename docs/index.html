Our story

A machine shop built over generations.

Karl Gschwind Machine Works began as a one-machine shop in Lynn, Massachusetts. Today, the business operates from a 12,000 square foot facility in Derry, New Hampshire.

Its long history is built on relationships with customers of many sizes and a commitment to producing high-quality precision machined parts.
